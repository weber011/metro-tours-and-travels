Common Questions

Everything you need to know about our services.

How do I book a taxi?

You can easily book a taxi by calling us directly, sending a WhatsApp message, or using the quick booking form on our website.

Do you provide airport pickup?

Yes, we offer reliable and timely airport pickup and drop-off services at affordable rates.

Are your drivers verified?

Absolutely. All our drivers undergo a strict background check and have years of professional driving experience to ensure your safety.

Can I book for outstation trips?

Yes, we provide outstation trip packages. We have transparent per-km billing and inclusive outstation packages for all our vehicles.

Do you provide wedding cars?

Yes, we provide premium, well-decorated cars for weddings and special events to make your day memorable.

What payment methods are accepted?

We accept all major payment methods including UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm), cash, and credit/debit cards.
